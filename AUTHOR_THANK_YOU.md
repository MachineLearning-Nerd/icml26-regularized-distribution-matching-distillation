# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Denis Rakitin, Ivan Shchekotov, and Dmitry Vetrov for making the RDMD paper and its source materials available. The paper gives a clear setting for studying one-step diffusion distillation, unpaired image translation, and transport-based correspondence, while the pinned equations and experiment descriptions make the reproduction boundary inspectable.

This repository credits the original work while distinguishing the authors’ reported results, pinned source artifacts, and the local non-reproducing toy. It does not claim author endorsement or constitute an official implementation.
